# Pure Run Leather Boots V05

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Pure Run 2D equipment inventory sprite

Create one pair of simple brown leather boots on a solid chroma-green background.

Reference roles:

- Approved leather armor V04: primary style anchor. Match its thick, crisp dark-brown outline, broad flat color regions, restrained two-step shading, and deliberately simplified hand-drawn shapes.
- Approved iron helmet V04: secondary style anchor. Match its low detail density and matte, non-reflective treatment.
- Diablo II screenshot: silhouette and presentation reference only. Use the staggered pair, with one boot in three-quarter side view and the other more front-facing. Do not copy its realistic rendering, material texture, lighting, or detail density.

Required design:

- A compact pair of mid-calf brown leather boots, visibly belonging to the same icon set as the approved leather armor and iron helmet.
- Chunky, readable silhouette with rounded toes and slightly irregular handmade construction.
- Only two or three broad construction details per boot: a cuff edge, one simple seam or wrap band, and at most three large lace marks.
- Thick clean outline of consistent weight. Interior lines must be equally crisp and intentional.
- Flat medium-brown base, one darker shadow shape, and at most one small muted lighter plane. Matte surface.
- The two boots overlap slightly and read clearly at 128 px.

Avoid:

- no painterly rendering, airbrush gradients, glossy highlights, rim lighting, bloom, ambient occlusion, or realistic cast shadow
- no leather grain, scratches, pores, dirt speckles, micro-wrinkles, dense stitching, dense lacing, or tiny buckles
- no sculpted 3D look, concept-art polish, photorealism, or AI fantasy-item rendering
- no thin fuzzy lines, soft edges, blur, excessive antialiasing, text, frame, pedestal, or extra objects

Composition:

- one centered equipment icon, generous green margin, complete silhouette, no crop
- solid uniform #00ff00 background
- no text and no watermark
